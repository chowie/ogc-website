We all need guidance in life...lots of it. But taking Aunt Millie's book suggestions or googling "depression" often leaves us more confused. Oneida wants to provide trusted, high quality resources for learning about God, ourselves and the Christian life.

## Browse and Borrow

- [Browse the church library catalog](https://www.libib.com/u/oneida)
- Request a book by emailing [office@oneidagospel.org](mailto:office@oneidagospel.org), or ask church staff on Sunday.
