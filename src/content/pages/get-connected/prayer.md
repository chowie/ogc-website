## Send a Prayer Request

Tell us how we can pray for you with the [prayer request form](/contact#prayer-request). You can leave your name off if you prefer.

## Pray With Us Every Day

Receive daily prayer requests for our church in one of three ways:

- **PrayerMate (preferred).** PrayerMate is a free prayer list for your phone. Each day features a new prayer card for the church. You can also add family members, friends and missionaries, and track prayer requests and answers.
- **Daily email.** A daily church prayer request delivered to your inbox.
- **Print copies.** A printed calendar of prayer needs.

[Subscribe to the prayer list](https://oneidagospel.elvanto.net/form/41e8657c-69eb-49f9-a7c2-d167e4d70a59). Members can also ask for the membership list to pray through.

## Shared Relationship Prayer

John, the apostle, called himself "the disciple Jesus loved" because he marveled that he was granted a relationship with God's Son. But his joy didn't stop there: it wasn't complete until he entered deep relationship with others who had the same relationship with Christ.

> "that which we have seen and heard we proclaim also to you, so that you too may have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ. And we are writing these things so that our joy may be complete." (1 John 1:3–4)

Oneida has set aside seasons to pray that, like John, we would more fully enjoy shared relationships with Christ and his people, so that people inside and outside the church experience the transforming love of Jesus. During those seasons we pray privately each day, join the elders and worship team before the service, and gather for an evening of prayer together.
