## What a Sunday Looks Like

Our worship service starts at **10:30 a.m.** every Sunday.

The sermon is expository. That means the pastor works through a book of the Bible, verse by verse, and explains what it meant then and what it means for us now.

From mid-September to May, Sunday School and classes for youth and adults meet at **9:15 a.m.**, before the service. You're welcome to come to both. After the service, a lot of people stay to talk. Linger as long as you like.

As a guest, you won't be asked to stand up, speak, or give anything. Just come and see.

## Your Kids

Kids are welcome in the service. At Oneida, children take part in worship as much as they're able.

- **Infants through kindergarten** are cared for from 10:15 a.m. to the end of the service.
- **1st–4th graders** go to Children's Church just before the sermon. You pick them up from their classroom after the service.
- **5th graders and older** stay with their families for the sermon. Kids' sermon notebooks are available.

Every children's volunteer has been screened and has a current background check. See [Kids & Families](/kids-and-families) for all age groups.

## Who You'll Meet

**Andrew Fulton** is our lead pastor. His mission is "to know the beauty of Christ and commend Him to all," and he loves sharing God's Word with others.

A few of our greeters will be pictured here with two lines each. Someone will be glad you came, and no one will put you on the spot.

## New to the Area?

Welcome to the Grand Ledge area. Moving is a lot of work, and finding a church is one more thing on the list. We'd love to help.

Once you've visited, join other newer attenders at **"Who Is Oneida?"**, a short class on our values and history. Pastor Andrew will introduce you to our church family and answer your questions.

It meets on a Sunday morning at 9:15, in the Meeting Room just off the Chapel. See the [events calendar](/events#calendar) for the next class.

## Coming Back to Church?

If it's been a while, welcome back. Most people step away for ordinary reasons: a move, a new job, a busy season. There's no pressure and no guilt here, just a seat saved for you.

## FAQ

**What time is the service?** Sundays at 10:30 a.m. Sunday School and adult classes meet at 9:15 a.m. from mid-September to May.

**Is there a nursery?** Yes. Infants through age 3 are cared for in Saplings, and ages 4, 5 and kindergarten in Mighty Oaks. Both run from 10:15 a.m. to the end of the service.

**Is the building accessible?** Details on accessible parking, entrances and restrooms will go here.

**Can I take communion?** A plain explanation of how Oneida observes communion will go here.

**How do I reach someone?** Call 517.627.7112 or email [office@oneidagospel.org](mailto:office@oneidagospel.org).

**What happens to my contact information?** If you fill out a form, a real person will reach out once. We won't add you to a mailing list or share your information.
