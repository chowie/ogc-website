## What a Sunday Looks Like

Our worship service starts at 10:30 a.m. and runs about 75 minutes. We sing a mix of hymns and newer songs, pray together, and hear a sermon that works through a book of the Bible. Most people dress casually; some dress up. Wear whatever you're comfortable in.

As a guest, you won't be asked to stand up, speak, or give anything. Just come and see.

## Your Kids

Kids are welcome in the service. During the sermon, children can go to classes for their age, and a nursery is open for little ones. Every volunteer who works with children has passed a background check. See [Kids & Families](/kids-and-families) for age groups and check-in.

## Who You'll Meet

A greeter will say hello at the door and help you find your way. Pastor name and a few greeters will be pictured here with two lines each. Someone will be glad you came, and no one will put you on the spot.

## New to the Area?

Welcome to the Grand Ledge area. Moving is a lot of work, and finding a church is one more thing on the list. We'd love to help. Join us for a newcomers' coffee after the service (date and details to come).

## Coming Back to Church?

If it's been a while, welcome back. Most people step away for ordinary reasons: a move, a new job, a busy season. There's no pressure and no guilt here, just a seat saved for you.
