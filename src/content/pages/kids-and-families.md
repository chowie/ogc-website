Oneida Kids helps parents with their most important job: raising children to worship and follow Jesus. Our classes teach, reinforce, and build on what you're already teaching at home.

Oneida Kids is led by **Rachel Fulton**, our Children's Ministry Director. Reach her at [children@oneidagospel.org](mailto:children@oneidagospel.org).

## Age Groups

| Group | Ages | When |
|---|---|---|
| Saplings (nursery) | Infants to age 3 | Sundays, 10:15 a.m. to the end of the service |
| Mighty Oaks | Ages 4, 5 and kindergarten | Sundays, 10:15 a.m. to the end of the service |
| Sunday School | Age 4 through 6th grade | Sundays, 9:15 a.m., mid-September to May |
| Children's Church | 1st–4th grade | Sundays, during the sermon |
| In the service | 5th grade and older | Sundays, 10:30 a.m., with family |
| Youth Group | Grades 5–12 | Sundays, 6–7:30 p.m. (2026–27 school year) |

**Saplings and Mighty Oaks.** Age-appropriate games and teaching, and loving arms to hold and rock babies.

**Sunday School.** In-depth Bible lessons for kids. Teachers use activities, lively conversation, and songs with motions to make Scripture easy to remember.

**Children's Church.** It's never too early to learn to worship joyfully, so kids join the service as much as they're able. Just before the sermon, 1st–4th graders go to their classrooms. They follow *The Biggest Story*, which traces Jesus' plan to fix everything sin has ruined, through songs, lessons, crafts, and questions that apply the gospel to their hearts.

**5th grade and older.** Older kids stay in the sanctuary to hear the sermon with their families. Sermon notebooks are available to help them follow along.

## Released Time Bible Class

Released Time lets public-school families choose to have their children excused from school for an hour of Bible teaching. Oneida offers this for families from **Strange School**, one hour on the **2nd Tuesday of each month** during the school year. Rachel Fulton leads it.

Kids learn *The Story of the Bible: A Pictorial Timeline of What God Is Doing in the Story of the Bible*. After a year, they can sing their way through the major events of the Bible and how to respond to them. Classes are interactive, with several teachers and flashcards for every student. [Read about the curriculum](https://music2myers.com/thestoryofthebible/).

[Sign up for email updates](https://oneidagospel.elvanto.net/form/51753af6-13de-475c-bb01-ba1f0cfcee5f) about the class and other opportunities for kids.

## Check-In and Safety

Your child's safety matters to us. Every children's volunteer has been screened and has a current background check.

In Children's Church, you pick up your child from their classroom after the service. Details on check-in for the nursery and preschool will go here.

Questions about our child protection policy? Contact Rachel Fulton at [children@oneidagospel.org](mailto:children@oneidagospel.org).
