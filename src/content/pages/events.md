## Community Events

These gatherings come around every year, and they're easy to invite a friend to.

### Vacation Bible School

Three nights for kids in kindergarten through 8th grade: **Monday–Wednesday, June 21–23, 2027, 6–8:15 p.m.** Middle and high schoolers have their own night on **Thursday, June 24, 6–8:15 p.m.** [Pre-register here](https://oneidagospel.elvanto.net/form/372e715d-e501-450d-99b2-794c735eafdf).

### Family Camp (August)

A weekend away together, Friday through Sunday, at Shady Green Pastures campground (5841 W. Gresham Hwy., Charlotte, MI). There's a bath house with showers, and small bunkhouses that sleep four can be reserved. Bring an RV, camper, or tent, or just come for the day. On Sunday we worship together at the park and celebrate baptisms.

### Ministry Fair (September)

When the fall schedule starts back up in September, a Ministry Fair after the service shows how you can get involved at Oneida and where you can serve.

### Trunk or Treat and Compete (October)

A fall gathering for families and neighbors. Details for this year will be posted here.

### Christmas Eve

We celebrate Christ's birth with a Candlelight Service of special music and readings. Bring your family and friends. This year's times will be posted here.
