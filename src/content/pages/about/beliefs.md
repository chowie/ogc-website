## In Plain Words

- **The Bible is God's Word.** It is true and without error, and it is our guide for every part of life.
- **God is one God in three persons.** He is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and he made everything that exists.
- **Jesus is God and man.** He lived without sin, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and is coming again.
- **We're saved by grace, not by what we do.** Forgiveness is God's gift through Jesus, not a reward for good works, baptism, or church membership. His Spirit lives in every believer and helps us grow to be more like Jesus.

## Our Full Statement of Faith

**The Bible.** The Bible is God’s Word, supernaturally inspired so that it is without error in the original texts and preserved by God in its verbal, complete inspiration; it is the absolute rule of truth for every age and every life. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)

**The Trinity.** God exists eternally in three persons - Father, Son, and Holy Spirit - and these three are one God. (2 Corinthians 13:14)

**Creation.** God is the absolute and sole creator of the universe, and that creation was by divine order. (John 4:24; John 1:3–4)

**God's Love.** God, by His sovereign choice and out of love for men, sent Christ into the world to save sinners. (John 3:16)

**Jesus Christ.** Jesus Christ in the flesh was both God and man, was born of a virgin and lived a sinless life, in which He taught and did mighty works and wonders and signs exactly as revealed in the Gospels. He was crucified, died for all mankind’s sins and was raised from the dead bodily on the third day. Later He ascended to the Father’s right hand where He is head of the church and intercedes for believers, and from where He is coming again personally, bodily, visibly to this earth to set up His millennial Kingdom. (Matthew 1:22–23)

**Christ's Sacrifice.** Because of His death and shed blood, the Lord Jesus Christ made a perfect sacrifice for sin, redeeming us from eternity in Hell. In turn, all men can be acquitted and cleared of sin on the simple and single ground of Jesus’ shed blood on the cross. (Isaiah 53:5)

**Salvation.** Salvation provides forgiveness of sins, and by it’s gifting us with a new nature we have the hope of eternal life which is entirely separate from our good works, baptism, church membership - or man’s effort - it’s pure grace. (Ephesians 2:8–9)

**New Life in Christ.** All who receive Christ take on the identity of Christ, and can find freedom from the bondage of sin. At death, our spirits depart to be with Christ and at the Lord’s return we are raised to dwell with Christ forever with new bodies. (Galatians 3:29)

**Growing in Christ.** It is the goal of every Christian to grow to become more like Christ through the power of the Holy Spirit. (John 14:15–17)

**The Holy Spirit.** The Holy Spirit is a person, is God, and possesses all the divine attributes. He indwells all believers and gives gifts to all believers as stated in I Corinthians 12. He baptizes and seals all believers at the moment of their salvation, and fills them in response to confession and repentance (I Corinthians 12:11). He anoints and empowers the believer to hear God’s heart for the sake of the Kingdom in accordance with His Word.
