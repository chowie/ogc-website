## In Plain Words

- **The Bible is God's Word.** It's true, and it shapes everything we do.
- **Jesus is who he said he is.** He lived, died for our sins, and rose again.
- **We're saved by grace.** Not by our good works, but by trusting Jesus.
- **God changes us.** His Spirit helps us grow to be more like Jesus.
