## What Membership Means

At Oneida, membership is a public commitment to this church family: to worship, serve, and care for one another, and to be cared for by the church's leaders. A plain explanation of what that commitment involves will go here.

## How to Join

1. Attend a newcomers' class.
2. Attend the membership class.
3. Meet with an elder.
4. Be welcomed into membership on a Sunday.
