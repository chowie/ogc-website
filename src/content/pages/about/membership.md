## What Membership Means

Before Jesus returned to heaven, he started the church to carry on his work. The church is alive and led by the Spirit. It's also organized, with leaders and members who belong to each other.

Membership is a public commitment to a local church. Like marriage, it's a formal promise: you commit to this church family, and you welcome the elders' care as they help you follow Jesus. Together, we show the world who he is.

If you follow Jesus and haven't joined a local church, we encourage you to take that step soon. We hope it will be Oneida.

## How to Join

1. **Come to "Who Is Oneida?"** This class for newer attenders covers Oneida's values and history. Pastor Andrew Fulton leads it and answers your questions. It's the first step before the membership class.
2. **Come to the membership class.** We'll walk through the beliefs, values, and practices that make Oneida who we are, and answer any questions you have.
