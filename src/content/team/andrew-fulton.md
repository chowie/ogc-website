> "My mission is 'to know the beauty of Christ and commend Him to all.' I am amazed by God's Word and treasure every opportunity I have to share it with others."

Andrew studied Bible and theology at Moody Bible Institute before teaching junior high and high school Bible classes. After completing his Master's degree in Biblical Exegesis at Wheaton College, he served as an Associate Pastor at New Covenant Church of Naperville, Illinois for 12 years.

Andrew enjoys golf, soccer, basketball, snowboarding, and computers. He and his wife, Rachel, have five children ages 19 to 9.
