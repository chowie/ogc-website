I worked for the Lansing School District for 34 years; as a teacher and administrator. I began attending Oneida as a small child and have enjoyed the longevity of relationships here.

I have continued to watch the Lord grow me and draw me closer in relationship with Him. As I continue to stumble He picks me up and shows me how much He loves me and wants me to depend on Him.

I have four children, four grandchildren, and a wonderful wife who are all a great encouragement as I strive to walk in the ways of our Lord Jesus Christ. Their love and prayers are a terrific support to me in my walk.
