I am retired from the United States Navy and am currently employed by the US Postal Service as an Electronic Technician. I came to salvation when about 14 or 15. While in the Navy I attended various Baptist churches and served as Deacon in the Southern Baptist church and here at Oneida. God then led me to serve as an elder.

I have a wife, Debbie, and three children: Amy, Kaylee, and Sam.
