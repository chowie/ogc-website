Hi! I can’t wait to meet you and your children!

I am a fellow Michigander, recently returned to this pure state after many years away. I am a mom of 5 great kids, have my degree in education and am a former teacher. I enjoy playing games, going on walks and hikes, losing myself in a book and being with family and friends!

I am enjoying my role as the Children’s Director at Oneida Gospel Church. We have had an exciting year planning, preparing, and teaching the children that God has brought to us! I love teaching God’s Word to young hearts. I pray that as they sing, play, craft and listen they would grow in their knowledge and love of our heavenly Father.

You can learn more about our ministry to children here ([link](https://www.oneidagospel.org/children)).
