My wife Linda and I have been married for almost 49 years and have been blessed with two children and four grand children. We have been attending OGC since May, 2001.

I was brought up in a Christian family environment and have been involved in church most of my life. My formal commitment to Christ was made in June of 1972 while visiting my brother-in-law and sister-in-law in Kentucky. My brother-in-law was an ordained minister and was instrumental in helping me with my decision. Through the years, I have continued with involvement in church leadership and committees. Since attending at Oneida, I continue to experience more growth in my Christian belief than at any other time in my life.

During our time at Oneida, I have served on the Missions Board for a total of 13 years and have now completed three terms as an Elder.

Serving on the Missions Board helped me expand my knowledge about missions abroad and locally and witness the impact on people’s lives. These past four years on the Board of Elders has increased my knowledge of church leadership and created an increased awareness of how important the Elder leadership is within the Oneida family.

I have immense respect for the leadership at Oneida, and I am humbled and honored to serve on the Board.
