Chris is Oneida's interim deacon chairman, approved by the Elders, and also serves as the deacon over technology and production. On Sundays that means the sound system, projection and presentation, and the rest of the AV that carries the service to the room and online.

During the week he looks after the church's website and digital presence, email and digital communications, and IT, from the network to researching new equipment.
