My wife (Jennifer) and I were married in 2007 and have been married for 18 years. We have five children: Elias, Evelyn, Edith, Elsie, and Ezekiel (Zeke). I am employed by Family Medicine of Michigan as a Registered Nurse. I also hold an FAA Certified A&P Aircraft Maintenance License. Jennifer takes care of “homebase” while homeschooling our children and working as the custodian for Oneida.

Our family has been attending Oneida Gospel Church since Palm Sunday (April 2) in 2023, and I have been leading corporate worship at Oneida for the last two years. I was approached by the nominating committee to serve as an Elder Intern at the end of 2024, and have completed the annual intern process.

Over the years, the Lord has called me to invest both professionally and personally in the spiritual health of His people. Most notably, instructing and directing people to draw closer to Him through personal trials and seasons of lament. I wish to continue Shepherding His flock using the skills and gifts He has given me, as well as, lead through corporate, personal worship, and prayer.
